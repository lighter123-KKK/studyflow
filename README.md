# StudyFlow → [OPEN THE WEBSITE](https://studyflow-presentation.vercel.app/)

**StudyFlow puts the dates from your classes and everyday life in one place. You can sync your CityU Canvas deadlines, add a date by hand, or upload a file. Got dates in a WhatsApp screenshot, an email attachment, or a photo of a noticeboard? Upload the file and StudyFlow can pick out several deadlines and events at once. That saves you from typing them one by one and lets you manage them all in one task list and calendar.**

The standard way to add a personal event in Canvas is to fill in an event form. StudyFlow adds the option to read dates directly from your files. [Canvas personal calendar guide](https://community.instructure.com/en/kb/articles/662788-how-do-i-add-an-event-to-my-personal-calendar) · [About StudyFlow](https://studyflow-presentation.vercel.app/about)

You can start right away without signing in to StudyFlow.

1. **Upload files.** Choose a PDF, PNG, JPG/JPEG or WebP. You can upload up to **6 files at once, 4 MB each, 24 MB total**. StudyFlow finds dates so you can add them without typing them one by one. [Try the sample poster](docs/hackathon-import-test.png). The site allows up to 20 file reads per IP address each hour.
2. **Add schedule.** Type a name, pick a date and select **Save task**. The new item appears in **My Tasks** and **Calendar**.
3. **Connect Canvas (optional).** To bring in your latest CityU Canvas assignments, select **Connect Canvas** and paste your own access token. The page shows you where to get one.

Just looking? Open **My Tasks** to try two editable examples, **Calendar** to see your dates, or **Group Projects** to try a sample team project.
